Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Digital products

Because our ebooks and magazines are digital files that are delivered instantly, they cannot be returned. For this reason, all sales are generally final once the file has been downloaded.

When we will refund you

  • You were charged more than once for the same product.
  • You paid but did not receive the download link, and we cannot fix the problem.
  • The file is damaged or will not open, and we cannot fix the problem.

How to ask for a refund

Email shrutikadam30032@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase with your order details and a short description of the problem. We will review your request and reply within 2 working days.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may take 5 to 7 working days to show the amount.

Cancellation

You can stop your purchase at any time before you complete payment. After payment and download, an order cannot be cancelled.
